# ASHA Master Prompt Specification

**Version:** 1.7

## 1. Mandatory Language & Personal Greeting

``` text
SELURUH RESPONS HARUS MENGGUNAKAN BAHASA INDONESIA.
```

-   English is allowed only where technically necessary, such as iCalendar (`.ics`) RFC 5545 syntax or technical identifiers.
-   When `personal.nickname` (`"What should I call you?"`) is provided, the Master Prompt includes a `Sapaan Panggilan` field and explicitly instructs the AI assistant to greet the user warmly using their nickname at the start of the response.

## 2. User Context Included in the Prompt

Every input provided by the user (or generated by ASHA's assumption/personalization engine) is serialized into the Master Prompt:

-   **Profil Pribadi**: `nickname` (`Sapaan Panggilan`), `age`, `sex`, `ethnicity` (`Asian`, `Kaukasian`, `American`, `Latin`, `Indian`, `Other`), `heightCm`, `weightKg`;
-   **Indikator Kesehatan (Otoritatif & Hasil OCR)**: blood pressure (`systolic/diastolic`), fasting glucose, uric acid, total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides, resting heart rate, medical conditions, medications, injuries/limitations, conditional physiological context (`menstrualOrPregnancyContext`), and OCR unreadable fields;
-   **Aspirasi, Target Bertahap & Jangka Waktu**: primary aspiration, staged target metric (`Muscle Mass %`, `Fat Percentage %`, `Target Weight Kg`, `Running Distance + Target Pace`, or `Health Indicator + Target Value`), and `timeframeWeeks`;
-   **Jadwal, Jenis Latihan, Peralatan & Pola Diet**: `trainingDays`, `restDays`, `trainingTypes` (`Cardio`, `Strength`, `Mobility & Flexibility`), `preferredTrainingTime`, `sessionDurationMinutes`, `equipment` (including `Tidak Ada (Gunakan Bodyweight)` and `Treadmil / Walking Pad`), `dietPattern`, `ifProtocol`, and `eatingWindow`;
-   **Preferensi Rencana & Kalender**: `planType`, `planStartDate`, `calendarProvider` (`Google`, `Outlook`, `Apple`), and `exerciseVisualMode`;
-   **Konteks Personalisasi Fisiologis & Asumsi Sistem**: `trainingConsiderations`, `nutritionConsiderations`, and disclosed `assumptions`.

## 3. Sex-Aware Personalization Block

Use:

``` text
SEX-AWARE PERSONALIZATION

Gunakan informasi jenis kelamin pengguna hanya sebagai salah
satu variabel kontekstual dalam personalisasi.

Jangan menggunakan jenis kelamin sebagai satu-satunya dasar
untuk menentukan latihan, intensitas, kebutuhan energi,
jumlah kalori, komposisi makanan, atau target kesehatan.

Pertimbangkan faktor fisiologis yang relevan dengan jenis
kelamin hanya jika memiliki relevansi terhadap tujuan,
kondisi kesehatan, usia, aktivitas, atau kebutuhan pengguna.

Untuk pengguna perempuan, pertimbangkan faktor seperti
kehamilan, pascapersalinan, menyusui, siklus menstruasi,
atau kesehatan tulang hanya jika informasi tersebut tersedia
atau relevan. Jangan mengasumsikan kondisi tersebut.

Untuk pengguna laki-laki, pertimbangkan faktor fisiologis
yang relevan hanya apabila didukung oleh informasi pengguna
dan relevan terhadap perencanaan.

Jangan membuat stereotip seperti:
- laki-laki harus menggunakan latihan lebih berat;
- perempuan harus menggunakan latihan lebih ringan;
- laki-laki membutuhkan diet tertentu;
- perempuan membutuhkan pembatasan kalori tertentu.

Dasarkan rencana terutama pada:
1. tujuan pengguna;
2. kondisi kesehatan;
3. usia;
4. ukuran tubuh;
5. tingkat kebugaran;
6. pengalaman latihan;
7. jadwal;
8. peralatan;
9. beban latihan;
10. pemulihan;
11. faktor fisiologis yang relevan.

Jika informasi penting tidak tersedia, jangan mengarang.
```

## 4. Detailed Day-by-Day Training Plan & Exercise Movement Guide

The prompt instructs the AI to generate a **detailed day-by-day schedule (Hari demi Hari)** for the entire target timeframe starting on `planStartDate`.

For each training session, include:

-   exact date & day of week (anchored to `planStartDate`);
-   session type (aligned with selected `trainingTypes`: `Cardio`, `Strength`, `Mobility & Flexibility`);
-   duration & scheduled time;
-   warm-up, main exercises, and cool-down.

For **every movement/exercise prescribed**, the AI must include a complete **Panduan Gerakan Latihan (Exercise Movement Guide)**:

1.  **Nama Gerakan**;
2.  **Fungsi / Manfaat Gerakan**;
3.  **Otot yang Dilatih** (primary and secondary muscles);
4.  **Repetisi** (sets, reps, duration, and rest interval);
5.  **Cara Melakukan Gerakan** (step-by-step execution cues, posture, breathing, and safety notes);
6.  **Contoh (Gambar Gerakan)** referencing external visual guides such as `https://darebee.com/workouts.html` or `https://darebee.com/exercises/` (with text-only fallback if a specific visual URL cannot be verified).

## 5. Detailed Day-by-Day Nutrition Requirements

The prompt instructs the AI to provide a **day-by-day Meal Plan** across the entire target timeframe including:

-   daily meals & meal timing (respecting `ifProtocol` and `eatingWindow` when Intermittent Fasting is selected);
-   portions & food choices compatible with `dietPattern`;
-   estimated energy (kcal), protein, carbohydrates, fats, fiber, and hydration guidance.

## 6. Integrated Planning

When `integrated` (Training + Meal Plan) is selected:

> Jangan menyusun rencana latihan dan rencana makan sebagai dua rencana
> yang berdiri sendiri. Optimalkan keduanya secara bersama-sama
> berdasarkan tujuan, beban latihan, waktu latihan, pemulihan, kebutuhan
> energi, dan pola diet pengguna.

## 7. Generative AI `.ics` Calendar Generation

The Master Prompt instructs the Generative AI to produce two separate, standards-compliant RFC 5545 `.ics` code blocks at the end of its response—one for `My Training Plan` and one for `My Diet Plan`—starting on `planStartDate` and compatible with the user's selected `calendarProvider` (`Google`, `Outlook`, or `Apple`).

## 8. Quality Control

Before final response:

``` text
☐ User greeted by nickname (if provided)
☐ Sex used contextually without stereotyping
☐ Relevant physiological factors considered; unknown states not invented
☐ Detailed day-by-day Training & Meal Plan covers full timeframe from Plan Start Date
☐ Training matches goal and selected Training Types (Cardio, Strength, Mobility & Flexibility)
☐ Equipment strictly respected (including Bodyweight-only or Treadmil / Walking Pad)
☐ Every exercise includes fungsi/manfaat, otot yang dilatih, repetisi, cara melakukan, and darebee.com visual reference
☐ Diet & IF eating window respected
☐ Separate RFC 5545 .ics blocks generated for Training and Diet
☐ Safety & assumptions disclosed in Bahasa Indonesia
```
